Community Guidelines

This network is home to every Mindvalley Expert Guild. Each one runs on the same idea: members are the content, and what you get out of the room depends on what you and everyone else puts into it. These guidelines apply across every Chapter.

What this room asks of you

Post your share On your assigned date, every month. It does not have to be polished, it has to be real: what you built, what worked, what you are stuck on.
Show up Attend the calls that come with your Guild, and engage while you are there.
Give as well as take Reply to other members' shares, offer feedback when asked, and help the room the way you want the room to help you.

Being part of this community

These are Mindvalley's standing community rules, they apply here the same as anywhere else on the platform.

Be respectful Treat each other with kindness and empathy, regardless of differing opinions or backgrounds. Ask before you assume, not everyone wants a hug, and not every share is an invitation for unsolicited advice.
No harassment No threats, discrimination, or derogatory remarks about anyone's race, gender, sexuality, religion, or other personal characteristics.
No unsolicited pitching Talking about your own business is part of being here, this room runs on real work. Turning a share or a reply into a sales pitch is not. If someone's interested in what you do, they will reach out.
No illegal activity Nothing that violates the law or basic ethical standards, wherever you are joining from.
Maintain privacy What gets shared in a Share or on a call stays there. Talk about what you learned all you want, just leave out names and identifying details unless you have permission.
Keep feedback constructive Feedback should help, not just criticize. This also is not a space to coach someone who did not ask for it, if you want to offer support, ask first and respect a no.
Be open minded People share their own frameworks, results, and positions here. Disagreement is fine, mockery or dismissiveness is not.
Keep some topics out Politics, religion, and other identity based debates stay out of the room, unless a call is explicitly framed around that topic. If one comes up unplanned, the Chair will redirect it.

If engagement slips

Missing shares or calls does not go unnoticed. Your Chair and Community Admin will reach out if you go quiet. Repeated disengagement is handled under the terms you agreed to when you joined, up to and including removal without refund.

The full Code of Conduct and membership terms sit in your signed agreement. This page is the plain language version of the same expectations.

A safe room

Treat other members the way you would want to be treated in a room where everyone is exposing real, unfinished work. No harassment, no discrimination, no using someone else's vulnerability against them.

If something feels off, tell your Chair or Community Admin directly. That is what they are there for.

Any of this unclear? Your Community Admin is one message away, right in your Chapter's group.
